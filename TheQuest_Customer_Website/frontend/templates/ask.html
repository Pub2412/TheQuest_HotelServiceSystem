Guest Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard check-in and check-out times?

Check-in commences at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late departures may be arranged through your in-room portal or by contacting the concierge.

What is the policy regarding "The Ridiculous Request"?

Our founding oath states: "We shall never deny a guest, even the most ridiculous request." From customized midnight banquets to unusual room staging, our concierge team will enthusiastically fulfill your needs provided they remain lawful and safe.

Can I extend my stay while currently checked in?

Yes! Currently checked-in guests can extend their stay by 1 to 7 nights directly from the Home or Profile page with instant card or room folio billing.
